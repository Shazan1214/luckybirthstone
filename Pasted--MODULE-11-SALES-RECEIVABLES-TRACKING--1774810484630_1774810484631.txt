==================================================
MODULE 11: SALES & RECEIVABLES TRACKING
==================================================

Create a centralized system to track all sales transactions and outstanding payments across buyers and approval partners.

----------------------------------
1. SALES RECORD SYSTEM
----------------------------------

Create “Sales Ledger”

Fields:

- id
- seller_id
- buyer_id
- partner_id (optional, if approval-based)
- listing_id
- gemstone_name
- quantity
- total_value
- currency
- sale_type (direct / approval)
- invoice_id
- created_at

----------------------------------
2. PAYMENT SUMMARY PER SALE
----------------------------------

Track:

- total_amount
- amount_received
- outstanding_amount

Auto-calculate:

outstanding_amount = total_amount - amount_received

----------------------------------
3. RECEIVABLES DASHBOARD
----------------------------------

Create “Pending Payments” view

Show:

- buyer name
- company
- total due
- overdue amount
- last payment date

Filters:

- All
- Pending
- Overdue
- Paid

----------------------------------
4. CUSTOMER LEDGER VIEW
----------------------------------

For each buyer/partner:

Show full history:

- all purchases
- payments made
- outstanding balance

----------------------------------
5. APPROVAL SALES TRACKING
----------------------------------

If sale_type = approval:

Track:

- owner_id
- partner_id
- final selling price
- partner margin

----------------------------------
6. PAYMENT ENTRY SYSTEM
----------------------------------

Allow manual entry of payments:

Fields:

- sale_id
- amount_paid
- payment_date
- payment_mode (cash, bank, online)
- notes

Support:

- partial payments
- multiple payments per sale

----------------------------------
7. OVERDUE CALCULATION
----------------------------------

If current_date > due_date:

Mark as:

- overdue

Show:

- days overdue

----------------------------------
8. AUTOMATED REMINDERS
----------------------------------

Trigger reminders for:

- pending payments
- overdue invoices

Channels:

- WhatsApp
- Email

----------------------------------
9. WHATSAPP REMINDER MESSAGE
----------------------------------

Template:

“Hello {{name}}, this is a reminder for pending payment of {{amount}} for {{gemstone}}. Kindly clear dues. – LuckyBirthstone”

----------------------------------
10. EMAIL REMINDER
----------------------------------

Subject:

Payment Reminder – Invoice {{invoice_id}}

Body:

Include:

- invoice details
- amount due
- due date

----------------------------------
11. UI COMPONENTS
----------------------------------

Create:

- Sales Ledger Table
- Receivables Dashboard
- Customer Ledger Page
- Payment Entry Modal

----------------------------------
GOAL
----------------------------------

Enable traders to track all sales, payments, and outstanding dues across direct and approval-based trades, with automated reminders to improve collections.
